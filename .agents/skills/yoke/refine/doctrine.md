# Refine — Critique Doctrine

Preserve concrete directory trees, layouts, naming choices, interfaces and
"stays/moves" decisions. They are approved intent, not prose to abstract.
Every original AC must retain its concrete substance; additions and harmless
renumbering are allowed. **User voice is verbatim:** numbered questions,
observations, screenshots, quotations and evidence remain word-for-word.

Interpret the item as a complete usable outcome. Surface missing wiring,
errors, cleanup, documentation and discovery; do not invent unrelated scope
or redesign purpose. Explicitly remove obsolete support infrastructure.
Docs/code after delivery describe the present. Hard cutover is simplest
unless live data/users/integrations prove graceful migration is needed.
**Permanent ordered migration history is retained**, even when data is empty.

Apply reuse, quality, efficiency and future-concept lenses. Shared-state
surfaces—actors/sessions/heartbeats, ownership/leases/claims, approvals/overrides,
evidence/run records/journals/packets, locks/route-around coordination—need
the smallest honest end-state v0 with current consumers and future non-goals,
or an explicit deletion/absorption target.

Artifacts cold-start downstream work: verify live references, add concrete
examples and actual search/residue guidance. Diagnose systemic causes,
not personal blame; inspect item events and note root causes for work-item
filing:
```bash
yoke events query --item "$ITEM_REF"
```

Think from purpose to requirements and from each detail to the whole result.
The checklist is a starting point; use judgment to expose missing outcomes.
